## What is in it

- a coastal distillery's brochure site: a home page, an about page, its bottlings with tasting notes, a comparison of bottlings, search, and a contact page;
- reusable highlights kept in the Library, and a highlight block (`components/blocks/highlight.tsx`);
- a contact form, photographs with their credits, and a stylesheet;
- the simple redirects bundle.

## Where to look first

`components/pages/` holds a view for each page type. `bunx crombolo site show` lists its types, forms and groups, and `bunx crombolo types show expression` shows how a bottling is put together.
