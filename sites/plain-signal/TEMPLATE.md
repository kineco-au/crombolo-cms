## What is in it

- a magazine about AI for everyone: sections, and articles with photographs and their credits;
- an approval workflow, where a sub-editor and then an editor sign an article off before it is published;
- a contact form drawn by an island, a part of the page that runs in the browser.

## Where to look first

`bunx crombolo workflows show articleApproval` shows the sign-off, and `components/shared/` holds the article list and the contact form island.
