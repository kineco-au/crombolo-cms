## What is in it

- a content page and a search page type;
- a header, a footer and a two-column page layout in `components/`, with placeholder text;
- a home page with About, Contact and Search pages beneath it.

## Where to look first

Restyle the Tailwind classes in `components/shared/` and the colours in `css/tailwind.css`, then replace the placeholder text in the back office or with `crombolo content update`.
