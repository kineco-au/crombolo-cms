## What is in it

- one content type, `homePage`, with a title;
- its view, `components/homePage.tsx`;
- one published page, the home page, as a content file in `content/pages/`.

## Where to look first

Open `components/homePage.tsx` and change what the page shows, then add types and pages from the command line: [A site from the command line](/docs/getting-started/from-the-command-line/) goes from here.
